Idoma land is known for farming and the production of different crops and food. Farming is an important part of community life, culture and livelihood.

🌱 What We Grow

Some of the crops and produce associated with Idoma communities include yam, cassava, plantain, orange, mango and other fruits, vegetables and food crops.

🍠 Yam

Yam is an important food crop in Idoma communities and is closely connected with farming, food and cultural life.

🌿 Cassava

Cassava is another important crop grown by farmers and used in different forms of food.

🍌 Plantain

Plantain is grown in many farming communities and is an important source of food.

🥭 Fruits

Fruits such as mango and orange are also part of the agricultural produce found in Idoma communities.
